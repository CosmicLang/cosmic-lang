## Result vocabulary

- **verified** — at least one direct, independently authored test exercises the family (or its refusal) and passes in the workspace suite.
- **partially verified** — the family is exercised only at family level, without per-production assertions.
- **failed** — an identified defect kept the construct from being verified.
- **intentionally divergent** — the accepted contract intentionally differs from the legacy behaviour.
- **not measured** — no independent repository test exists and the reason is recorded.
- **refused by design** / **rejected by design** — the accepted contract is the refusal (PARSE001) or the rejection; the refusal/rejection itself is verified.

## Notes

- **Granularity caveat.** Mapping is at family granularity. A row's result is the family result; families F06 and F13 are the only ones not holding a direct per-family integration test, and they are marked accordingly (F06 receives A3 corpus coverage, F13 stays partially verified).
- **Intentional divergences.** F05 covers the strict escape refusal (LEX008 / D-L8), the triple-quote refusal (LEX009 / D-L6) and the surrogate rules (LEX010 / D-L9); those divergences are declared in the decision packages and measured by the A3 probes.
- **Refusals.** The ten UNSUPPORTED productions (F09, F14, F15, F18) must be refused (PARSE001) until recognition is separately accepted; the refusal tests listed above are the verification.
- **No new acceptance.** This document introduces no acceptance: every recognised family was already accepted by the G1-L and G1-S decision packages and implemented in increments A1/A2.
- **Regeneration.** `python3 tools/a3-traceability.py` (stdlib only, offline). The generator fails when the map shape, the classification counts, or any evidence reference is invalid.
